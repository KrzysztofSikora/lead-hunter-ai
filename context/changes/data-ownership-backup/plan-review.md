## Decisions the PR reviewer should look at
- D-1: a new `deleted_usage` table keeps the cap honest. It is costly, it touches the budget queries of S-05/S-07, and alternative D (a full ledger) is the cleaner long-term shape.
- D-4: retention keeps rows that have a payment. This narrows PRD FR-21 AC3 ("they are deleted").
- D-8: export shape and versioning (`version: 1`) is a costly contract. Generic or allowlisted columns, see finding 3.
- D-3: dedup identities are deleted, so deleted or retained items can come back as new.

## What is good
- The findings are well grounded. The cascade map, the claim that each opportunity owns one raw signal (`ingest.ts:63-104`), and the observation that limits are summed from stored rows all check out against the code.
- D-2 catches the real race window (an `await` on the provider call, then a save into a deleted row) and keeps the lesson on billed answers.
- Retention runs only after a successful backup, rotation never touches foreign files, and the copy is written as `.partial` with `quick_check`. These are sound defaults.
- The DST cases for the 03:30 cron are correct (02:30Z on 2026-10-25, 01:30Z on 2027-03-28).
- The phases are small, each can be verified on its own, and the agent checks come from the Gates table, with Docker gates only in phase 5.
